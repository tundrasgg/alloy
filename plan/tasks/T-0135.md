## Goal

No initiation that `alloy-link` accepted once is accepted again, whatever happened to its sender's key in between (ARFC 0003 section 3.2).

## Scope

- Read only ARFC 0003 section 3.2 and ARFC 0002 section 3.3 step 4, and the review.
- Resolve finding F1 of `plan/reports/T-0127.reviewer.1.md`. A key that was a peer, was removed, and then had one unknown initiation accepted moves into `transient`. Its replay entry can then be evicted, so an initiation captured earlier is accepted again. Keep the replay floor for such a key, or bound eviction so it never drops a floor that still guards an accepted counter. Turn the reviewer's scratch test into a committed test.
- Fix F2, the misplaced doc comment on `wipe` and `source_address`.

## Out of scope

- Anything else in `alloy-link`.
