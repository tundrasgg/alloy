## Goal

`alloy-api` serves exactly what ARFC 0002 section 3.15 gives rung 2:
- the API port: `ListNodes` and `ExchangeLoginCode`, with `IssueLoginCode` refused;
- the local admin socket: `ListNodes` and `IssueLoginCode`, with `ExchangeLoginCode` refused, and callers authorized by `peer_uid`;
- every other method: `UNIMPLEMENTED`.

The generated client covers the CLI's rung 2 calls.

## Scope

- Read only ARFC 0002 sections 3.7 and 3.15, ARFC 0007 clause 3.10, ARFC 0008 clauses 3.7 and 3.7.2, and ARFC 0019 clauses 3.1.11 and 3.1.14.
- The `IssueLoginCode` and `ExchangeLoginCode` definitions in `proto/`, documented. The handlers run over `alloy-state` through trait arguments.
- The local admin listener on the Unix socket that ARFC 0002 names, authorizing by `peer_uid` (T-0129), fail closed.
- Fold T-0118 re-review F1 (D-0060): an evicted unproven connection is closed at once, not at its close deadline.
- Tests: each method on each listener, the refusals, `UNIMPLEMENTED` with no `alloy-error-bin` trailer, and an exchange from a code issued on the local admin socket.

## Out of scope

- `alloyd` wiring (T-0132), and other methods.
