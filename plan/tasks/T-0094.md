## Goal

`alloy-sim`'s `mem` `disk-io` implements ARFC 0005 clauses 3.1.10 to 3.1.13 and the disk faults of 3.3.4 and 3.3.7, replacing the `Unsupported` placeholder T-0076 left.

## Scope

- As option 1 of the question in `plan/reports/T-0076.developer.1.md` lists:
  - the namespace, files, and block devices (3.1.10, 3.1.11, 3.1.13);
  - durability at a crash under `Unsynced` `Lost`, `Seeded`, and `Torn` (3.1.12 and the disk part of 3.3.4);
  - capacity and read faults (3.3.7);
  - `HostConfig::{devices, capacity, block_size}`, `DeviceConfig`, `DiskTarget`, `Fault::{Disk, Capacity}`, and the `disk-op` records.
- Fold finding F1 of `plan/reports/T-0076.reviewer.2.md` (D-0043): no two `rng` instances in one run draw the same stream unless an `rng_replay` configures it, including the extra instances of two replays of one start. Add a regression test.
- Run the `disk-io` suite of `tests/conformance/rt` against `mem` through the harness T-0076 added.

## Out of scope

- Networks (T-0077).

## Acceptance

```
cargo test -p alloy-sim --locked   # expected: passes, including the disk-io conformance cases against mem
cargo xtask ci                     # expected: exits 0
```
