## Goal

ARFC 0005 states when the timer of `Runtime::timeout` starts and what `RtPath::join` accepts, so the runtime conformance suite can pin both.

## Scope

- Clause 3.1.14: the timer of `timeout` starts at the call or at the first poll; say which and why.
- `RtPath::join`: whether a component may contain `/`, and the error otherwise.
- The exact `Debug` text of `Datagram`, `RecvSlot`, and `IoBuf` (section 2.1 says "lengths only"), stated as ARFC 0001 clause 3.18 states its own (`plan/reports/T-0069.developer.1.md`).
- From `plan/reports/T-0050.reviewer.2.md`: F1, bytes the accepting end writes before the connecting end is live are not lost; F2, the abandoned setup reset is sent only when no reset already went in that direction; note F3, an observer panic on `run-end` itself.
- Prefer the readings `alloy-rt` implements (`plan/reports/T-0007.developer.1.md`) unless the spec gives a reason against them; say which in the report.

## Out of scope

- The simulator world (T-0050).

## Acceptance

```
grep -n -i "first poll\|at the call" spec/arfc-0005-simulator-and-natlab.md   # expected: the timeout start is stated
```
