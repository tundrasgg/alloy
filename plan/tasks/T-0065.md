## Goal

ARFC 0007 states every way the break-glass local admin can reach tenant data, so no claim in sections 3.3 or 8 overstates the boundary.

## Scope

- F1 (`plan/reports/T-0052.reviewer.2.md`): section 8's "there is no third way" and clause 3.3's claim that the local admin's access to tenant data is always in the org's audit log miss the cluster path (placing an org's guests and volumes on a node it controls). State that path and what records it, or bound it.
- Note F2: clause 3.10.7's list of what may still refuse the recovered key includes the default deny of clause 3.2 for targets in another org, or says why not.

## Out of scope

- Reducing the local admin's power (D-0020).
