## Goal

ARFC 0003 sections 3.4 and 3.5 leave no byte-level choice open for the fragment header and transport queue ids.

## Scope

- State whether fragment `index` counts from 0 or 1, which inner kinds a fragment may carry, and that the fragment header's `reserved` byte is zero and what a receiver does otherwise.
- State whether transport queue ids count from 0 below the number of queues, and what a receiver does with one out of range.
- Prefer the readings `alloy-wire` already implements (`plan/reports/T-0054.developer.1.md`, Decisions) unless the spec gives a reason against them; say which in the report.

## Out of scope

- Any change to header sizes.
