## Goal

The control-plane half of M11 rung 2 can be built from the specs:
- the cluster state store with its consensus group of one voter;
- node records as resources;
- heartbeats and a defined `healthy`;
- `alloy node list` output.

T-0102 specifies identity, join, transport, and ports in parallel.

## Scope

- The state store for M11: confirm or replace ARFC 0006 section 5's pending defaults (`openraft`, `redb`) from R-0004, and give the `log-store` and `state-store` seam interfaces enough detail that a developer and a verifier can work in parallel, as ARFC 0005 section 2 does for `alloy-rt`. Record the choice in ADR 0064.
- What rung 2 needs from ARFC 0006 and nothing more:
  - one group running both global and regional state on one voter (clause 3.2: one or two control nodes give one voter);
  - linearizable writes and reads;
  - generation checks;
  - snapshots, enough to survive an `alloyd` restart.
  Mark the rest of ARFC 0006 as later.
- Heartbeats and health:
  - what a node sends and how often, over which path in rung 2 (T-0102 decides underlay versus mesh; align with it through the shared rung 2 goal, and say what changes in rung 3);
  - when a node is `healthy`, `unreachable`, or `revoked`, consistent with the existing 30 s `unreachable` rule;
  - what a client node reports.
- `alloy node list` in ARFC 0019: columns (name, roles with `-` for a client node, zone, status, release, addresses), order, `--output json`, and the T-rules of `product/principles.md` that apply.
- Audit: what rung 2 must record per ARFC 0018 for `init`, `join`, and node record changes, at the smallest level that keeps ARFC 0008 clause 3.7 true.

## Out of scope

- Identity, join, transport, ports (T-0102).
- Controllers other than the node record lifecycle.
- Watches beyond what heartbeats need.
- Multi-voter membership changes (later rungs).
