## Goal

A sans-I/O crate, `alloy-wire`, encodes and decodes every AL message whose bytes ARFC 0003 sections 3.1, 3.4, and 3.5 fix: initiation, response, cookie reply, transport header, inner frame, and fragment header. It performs no cryptography and no I/O.

## Scope

- Typed structs for message types 1, 2, 3, and 4, the inner frame, and the fragment body, with `encode` into a caller buffer and `decode` from a byte slice that returns a typed error and never panics on any input.
- Enforce every fixed value and drop rule section 3.1 states (version, reserved bytes, flags, exact lengths). Ciphertext, MACs, and AEAD tags are opaque byte arrays; computing or checking them belongs to `alloy-link` and `alloy-crypto`.
- The fragment header fields of section 3.5 and their bounds (2 to 16 fragments). Reassembly logic is out of scope.
- Constants for the per-packet overhead section 3.4 states.
- `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, no dependencies outside `std` unless `agent/eng/deps.md` allows them; add the crate to the workspace and to `deny.toml`.
- Unit tests, including round-trip property tests and a test that every truncation and every single-byte change of a valid message decodes to an error or to a different valid value, never a panic.

## Out of scope

- Probe messages (type 5): their layout is under review in T-0038.
- Relay envelopes and the stream underlay framing.
- Conformance vectors (T-0055).
