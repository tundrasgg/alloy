## Goal

PB-0001 describes exactly what a person types and sees from installing Alloy to a second node joined and a first login from another machine.

## Scope

- The full transcript: install, `alloy init`, its output, `alloy join`, its output, `alloy login` from a laptop, `alloy node list`.
- Every prompt, default, and message, including the single anchor warning.
- The error table for the ten most likely failures: unreachable address, expired token, wrong token, anchor address changed, port in use, no virtualization, already joined, version skew, approval pending, lost admin credential.
- Requested design changes to ARFC 0002 and ARFC 0019, if any.

## Out of scope

- Commands beyond init, join, login, token, and node.
- Console screens.
