## Goal

ARFC 0019 and ARFC 0008 say enough for the console to be built against the real API: every entry of PB-0002's "Requested design changes" is resolved in a spec.

## Scope

- Resolve each requested design change of PB-0002:
  - browser authentication, including the console bridge's one-time launch path and session token;
  - the browser wire form of the API (R-0007);
  - serving the static files from `alloyd` and pinning on the bridge;
  - watches for live state.
- ADR 0070 records the console's toolchain: TypeScript, Vite, and Preact, chosen by the lead in D-0066 and used by T-0140. State the rules that keep the console small and fast (ARFC 0019 section 7), and how `cargo xtask` checks it. Add the npm dependency rules to `agent/eng/deps.md`.

## Out of scope

- Building the console (T-0140), and the bridge in `alloy`.
