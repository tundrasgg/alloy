## Goal

Answer: which crates should back consensus, the embedded state store, and the cryptographic primitives, under the rules in `agent/eng/deps.md`?

## Scope

- Consensus: evaluate `openraft` and at least one alternative for sans-I/O use, membership change, snapshots, and simulator fit.
- Embedded store: evaluate `redb` and at least two alternatives for crash safety, license, and write amplification.
- Cryptography: confirm each crate in `agent/eng/crypto.md` for license, audits, maintenance, and `no_std` or constant-time notes, and whether a Noise library supports the handshake in ARFC 0003 with XChaCha20-Poly1305 or whether it must be assembled from primitives.
- The dependency report fields from `agent/roles/researcher.md` for every candidate.

## Out of scope

- Benchmarks beyond published numbers.
- Choosing: the note recommends, an ADR decides.
