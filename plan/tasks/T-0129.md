## Goal

`alloy-rt` provides the two operations T-0126 added to ARFC 0005:
- `Stream::peer_uid`, the peer credentials of a Unix domain socket;
- `disk-io`'s durable `replace`.

Both are implemented under `os` and `mem`. `alloy-id`'s atomic replacement uses `replace`, which closes T-0106 review F4.

## Scope

- Read only ARFC 0005 sections 2.3 and 2.4 and the clauses of 3.1 that T-0126 added for `peer_uid` and `replace`, plus ARFC 0002 section 3.14.
- `os`: `peer_uid` through `SO_PEERCRED` on Linux, and on macOS what the clause states. On Windows, what the clause states: the client node needs none. `replace` uses `rename` plus the stated directory sync on Unix, and `MoveFileExW` with `MOVEFILE_REPLACE_EXISTING | MOVEFILE_WRITE_THROUGH` on Windows.
- `mem` in `alloy-sim`, with trace records.
- `alloy-id`'s `DataDir` replaces files through `replace`. Fold T-0121 review F4: the doc comment states the clause it relies on, not an unsourced claim.
- Tests on each platform. The lead runs Linux.

## Out of scope

- The local admin listener (T-0131).
