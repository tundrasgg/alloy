## Goal

ARFC 0003 section 6 states a handshake cost limit that an `IKpsk2` responder can meet.

## Scope

- Section 6 allows "one DH per packet when not under load". An IK responder needs two DH operations to process message 1 (`es`, `ss`) and two to build message 2 (`ee`, `se`). Restate the limit in terms of handshakes or DH operations per handshake message, keeping the denial-of-service protection the section intends.
- Section 3.1 cookie lifetime (finding F1 in `plan/reports/T-0026.reviewer.2.md`): the initiator's keep window and the responder's acceptance window are made consistent with network delay accounted for, or the text states the guarantee that actually holds.
- If the change alters required behavior, report `blocked` with the ADR number you need. A clarification needs no ADR.

## Out of scope

- Other sections of ARFC 0003.
