## Goal

ARFC 0003 section 3.6 defines probe messages byte for byte, and an underlay attacker cannot replay a probe, as ARFC 0020 section 3.2 requires.

## Scope

- Plaintext layout of probe and probe answer messages: the 16-byte challenge and 16-byte echo that ARFC 0004 section 3.2 step 4 now requires, a fixed unpadded length, and padding for path MTU probes.
- Replay protection for probes: today they carry no counter, so ARFC 0020 section 3.2 is not met on main. Specify a counter, a per-probe-set token, or another mechanism with a stated window, and its cost under the section 6 limits.
- Whether a probe's static-static key is computed per packet or once per peer, consistent with the section 6 limit for probes under load.
- Write ADR 0046 if the replay mechanism is a new decision, and index it.

## Out of scope

- ARFC 0004 changes (T-0039).
- Sections of ARFC 0003 other than 3.6 and 6.
