## Goal

A compromised anchor without `control` cannot change another node's control plane state through forwarded join service requests. The node channel rules have an ADR. D-0050 merged T-0105 with these findings open.

## Scope

- Read only ARFC 0002 sections 3.5, 3.9, 3.13, and 8, ARFC 0006 clauses 3.13 and 3.14, ARFC 0018 clause 3.3, and ARFC 0003 section 3.2.
- Resolve every finding of `plan/reports/T-0105.reviewer.1.md`:
  - F1 (block): forwarded `withdraw` and replays naming another joiner's key, and the false `renew` replay claim;
  - F2, F3, and F4 (fix). For F4, record the node channel change in ADR 0065, superseding or amending ADR 0042 as `agent/protocol/decisions.md` requires;
  - F5 (note).
- Prefer the simplest rule that holds, for example a joiner-generated proof bound into every forwarded request, or anchors forwarding only the first join request.

## Out of scope

- Anything not named by those findings.
