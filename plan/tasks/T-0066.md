## Goal

ARFC 0001 leaves no gap that blocks the conformance suite for `alloy-seam`, and its stored-binding rules hold for every group, every fallback, and every write during finalization.

## Scope

- From `plan/reports/T-0057.reviewer.2.md`: F1 (block), a regional group created after cluster creation holds write versions and refuses bindings at any other version, including during an upgrade; F2, a node that fell back to `R - 1` before the switch is handled; F3, the check also re-encodes, so a binding that cannot be written at `V(R)` fails the check, not the rewrite; F4, name a check that exercises the check, switch, rewrite, completion, and ordering rules; F5, a write that races the switch is re-encoded and retried, or reported as retryable, stated once; note F6, `same` under a non-reflexive `PartialEq`.
- Carry the matching ARFC 0017 changes: an upgrade to `R + 1` refuses until `R` is finalized, and `--finalize` includes the rewrite (the T-0057 follow-ups listed in `plan/STATE.md`).
- From `plan/reports/T-0007.developer.1.md`: the `Display` impls of `Kind` and `ImplName`; what `instantiate` returns for an invalid `S::KIND`; the precedence of errors in clauses 3.10 and 3.11 (`TooLong` against `InvalidByte`, `KindConflict` against `Duplicate` against `Full`).
- ADR 0059 if any rule is a new decision; ADR 0056 is accepted and not edited.

## Out of scope

- ARFC 0005 (T-0067).
