## Goal

`alloy-state` holds the part of the control plane that rung 2 needs on top of T-0107's seams and group:
- the `Node` kind of ARFC 0006 section 2.6;
- status messages applied as soft state (section 4.3);
- node state (clauses 3.13 to 3.15).

`alloyd` can then record nodes and their health.

## Scope

- Read only ARFC 0006 sections 2.6, 3.13 to 3.15, and 4.3, and T-0107's report.
- The `Node` kind's fields and their validation, created and updated through T-0107's resource API.
- Status messages as soft state, held in memory with leases (section 2.3) and never written through the group. Node state follows the rules of clause 3.15: `healthy`, `unreachable`, and the others, with the leader-change rule.
- Tests: node create and update with a generation; status updates moving a node between states on a simulated clock; a leader restart showing the clause 3.15 behavior.

## Out of scope

- The node channel and `alloyd` (T-0111).
