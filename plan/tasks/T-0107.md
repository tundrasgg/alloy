## Goal

`alloy-state` provides the cluster state of M11 rung 2:
- resources, `Node` among them;
- the `log-store` and `state-store` seams of ARFC 0006 section 5, with `native` implementations on `redb` and `mem` implementations;
- one `openraft` group, linearizable writes with generation checks, and snapshots that survive an `alloyd` restart.

## Scope

- Read only ARFC 0006 sections 2, 3, 4, and 5, and ADR 0064.
- The `log-store` and `state-store` traits and their registration in `alloy-seam`, exactly as section 5.1 specifies. Then:
  - `native` implementations on `redb`, with file I/O on the data directory;
  - `mem` implementations;
  - the version 1 configurations of section 5.2.
- `openraft` wired to these stores, with time, tasks, and randomness through `alloy-rt` (R-0004's note: show that every timer and spawn goes through the trait). One group for one voter (section 2.8), with the snapshots of clause 3.10.
- Resources and the `Node` kind of section 2.6:
  - create, get, list, update with a generation, delete;
  - the state export format of section 4.1;
  - the status messages of section 4.3 applied as soft state.
- Add `openraft`, `redb`, and their trees to `deny.toml`, giving licenses in the report.
- Unit tests, plus a restart test: write, stop, reopen, read.

## Out of scope

- More than one voter, membership changes, watches beyond what the node lifecycle needs, and the API server.
