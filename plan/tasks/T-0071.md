## Goal

ADR 0060 records the pilot's answer to a licensing question: the NCSA license is allowed for `libfuzzer-sys` only, and `agent/eng/deps.md` names the exception.

## Scope

- ADR 0060, status `accepted`: `libfuzzer-sys` may be used under its declared `(MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND NCSA` license, in fuzz targets only; no other crate may use NCSA; fuzz binaries are never shipped. Alternatives the pilot rejected: allowing NCSA globally, and replacing libFuzzer.
- `agent/eng/deps.md`: one line naming the exception and its scope, citing ADR 0060.

## Out of scope

- `deny.toml` (T-0061 round 2 adds the exception there).
