## Goal

Within every bound ARFC 0004 states, a probe set never makes a working path's check missed unless that check or its echo is lost or overtaken. A re-verified path fails only on three consecutive misses of its own checks. Section 8 and ADR 0061 then hold as written.

## Scope

- Resolve every finding of `plan/reports/T-0080.reviewer.2.md`:
  - F1 (block): checks no longer share the probe challenge table with probe-set probes, or another rule keeps them from being evicted. Give the new or changed table its bound and its behavior at the bound in section 7 (I-21).
  - F2 (fix): the fail rule counts only checks sent since the far endpoint last became verified.
  - F3 (note): step 6 states the same fail condition as section 3.4, or defers to it.
  - F4 (note): section 8's move rule and section 3.6's switch to `stream` agree.
- Rerun the class-count model if any rule it depends on changes, and quote the result.

## Out of scope

- Anything not named by those findings.
