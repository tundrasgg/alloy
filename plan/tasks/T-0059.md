## Goal

No process ever seals under a key and nonce another process already used, and no message is accepted twice, across a failed process handover; and probes sealed at a rekey reach a peer that has not finished the handshake.

## Scope

- F1 (block in `plan/reports/T-0038.reviewer.2.md`): when a handover fails after the successor has used a session, the old process must not resume that session's send counters or receive windows, for transport (section 3.4) and probes (section 3.6). For example: the successor's first use of a session marks it consumed in handed-over state, and a resuming old process discards every consumed session and re-handshakes. State the rule in ARFC 0003 and the matching step in ARFC 0017 section 6, and check it against I-07 and ARFC 0020 section 3.2.
- F2: at a rekey, the responder keeps sealing probes and answers under the old session until it sees the initiator use the new one, or another rule under which a peer that has not received the response can still open them.
- ADR 0057 if the handover rule is a new decision.

## Out of scope

- Other handover state.
