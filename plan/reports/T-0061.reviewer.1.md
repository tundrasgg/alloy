## Summary

Reviewed `task/T-0061` at `ec4d400`. `cargo xtask ci` passes on the tip and on a trial merge with main. Scope is inside `touches`.
All four targets ran 120 seconds each with no finding. All 18 seeds decode, and the seed writer reproduces them byte for byte.
A mutation check shows each target is not vacuous: with one validity check disabled per decoder, each target fails within 60 seconds.
One `fix` finding: the shared `deny.toml` now lets a product crate depend on `libfuzzer-sys` and the fuzz-only build crates. ADR 0060 forbids that dependency, and the gate on main rejects it.

Attack 3, decoders the targets do not reach. `crates/alloy-wire/src` has eight public `decode` functions. Four have their own target. The other four, `Initiation`, `Response`, `CookieReply` and `Transport`, are reached through `Message::decode`. That function passes the whole input to the decoder its type byte selects (`message.rs` lines 69 to 78), and each of those decoders checks its own type byte (`exact_header` and `min_header`). The `message` target therefore covers them, and the mutation in `take_reserved` was caught through it. No gap was found.

Banned list, checked by reading `fuzz/`: no `todo!`, `unimplemented!`, `unreachable!`, `#[ignore]` or `unsafe`. Each `expect` in `fuzz/examples/write_seeds.rs` has a comment above its statement that states the invariant.
