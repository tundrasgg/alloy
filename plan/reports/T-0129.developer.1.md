## Summary

`Stream::peer_uid` and `DiskIo::replace` are in `alloy-rt` and implemented under `os` and `mem`. `os` `peer_uid` returns the `SO_PEERCRED` uid on Linux, `InvalidInput` on TCP, and `Unsupported` on other Unix platforms. `os` `replace` is `rename(2)` plus a directory `fsync` on Unix, and one write-through `MoveFileExW` on Windows. Under `mem`, `replace` makes its rename and the create of `from` durable on their own and writes `disk-op` 17. `DataDir::write` uses `replace`, which closes T-0106 F4, and its doc comment now cites clause 3.1.16 (T-0121 F4). Everything was run on Windows only. The Linux `peer_uid` and Unix `replace` paths have not been compiled or run.

## Decisions

## Issues

- spec-issue: ARFC 0005 clause 3.1.16 (`mem`) forces only the rename and the create of `from`. When `from`'s name was freed by an unsynced remove, or reached by an unsynced rename, a crash drops the forced create or the forced rename. That contradicts "after any later crash `to` names the file". `DataDir::write` avoids it by syncing after a stale-file removal.
