## Summary

ARFC 0003 now gives every session one lifetime: 3.3 rules 1 to 3 discard the whole
session (all keys, counters, windows, blocks) at once, so probe keys can no longer
outlive or underlive transport keys. Probe messages go under the session VNI 0
transport goes under, and the 5 s bound of 3.4 ("qualifies") covers probes and
binds. 3.9 passes the timers and order that 3.3 and 3.4 need. F3 (the ADR) is open.

## Decisions

- N: one lifetime per session (rules 1 to 3 of 3.3) for all keys, replacing the separate probe key retention of 3.6; the probe proof is then the transport proof.
- N: probe messages use the VNI 0 transport session choice, and the 5 s bound ("qualifies") applies to every message, binds and probes included; the VNI 0 fallback to the newest held session applies when no session both qualifies and is confirmed, so a late confirmation of an older session cannot pull sealing back to it.
- N: after a failed handover the old process discards every session with a peer it handed a consumed session for, and re-handshakes within 1 s as 3.9 already requires.
