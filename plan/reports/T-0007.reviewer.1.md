2. Live swap under faults (clauses 3.14 to 3.16). Each seed runs 4 consecutive swaps on one slot. Every swap method is scripted randomly as ok, fail, hang, or pending 1 to 4 polls. Prepare fails randomly (unknown name, factory refusal). The deadline fires at a random poll, and the future is dropped at a random poll on 1 in 6 swaps. After every pending poll the harness asserts that the slot is never torn. After each swap it asserts: the generation increments only on `Ok`; the slot is unchanged on every error; `Prepare` never quiesced; a `Prepare` deadline called nothing; `Failed` and `Deadline` resumed the old instance; no method was called on a retired instance; the deadline is polled at most once after it completes and never after it returns `Ready`; a dropped running future poisons the slot, and every later swap returns `Poisoned` and touches nothing.
3. Thread contention (clause 3.15 step 0, section 7 bound of 1 concurrent swap). 8 threads × 2,000 swaps on one slot, each polled to completion. The harness asserts that at most one future is past step 0 at any time and that the final generation equals the count of `Ok` results.
