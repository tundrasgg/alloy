## Decisions

- R: I classify F3 as `fix`, not `block`. The attack needs the victim's browser to load the attacker's page at a port, and then a later bridge to bind that same port. The cookie design of round 1 was open to the same service worker, which could read the secret from the old `/-/launch/<secret>` path.

## Issues

- spec-issue: I confirm the architect's ARFC 0006 issue. Non-decreasing global commit times are now required by ARFC 0007 3.12.4 but are not stated in `spec/arfc-0006-control-plane.md`.
- follow-up: ARFC 0019 names no CLI command or console control that calls `RevokeUserSessions` (`spec/arfc-0019-cli-and-console.md`). The remedy that ARFC 0007 section 8 offers a user is reachable only through the raw API.
