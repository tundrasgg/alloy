## Summary

Resolved the three `fix` findings of `plan/reports/T-0026.reviewer.1.md` on `task/T-0026`, after merging main into it.
Section 3.3 now states the binding property the construction has (F1) and requires erasure of all secret handshake state, chaining key and ephemeral private key included (F2).
Section 3.1 now fixes every handshake byte (F3): sizes of all three message types, `sender_index` and `receiver_index` use, reserved and flag values with drop rules, `mac1` and `mac2` inputs, the cookie, and the cookie reply (type 3).
ADR 0043 is retitled to cover the whole handshake decision and re-indexed. The reviewer was right on all three findings.

## Changes

- F1 (fix, resolved): `spec/arfc-0003-mesh-wire-protocol.md` 3.3 "Properties" says the keys are bound to both static keys, both ephemeral keys, both payloads and the psk, and are not bound to `type`, `version`, `reserved`, the indices, `mac1` or `mac2`; 3.1 says the fields outside Noise are unauthenticated. ADR 0043 states the same, and records why the prologue does not bind them.
- F2 (fix, resolved): 3.3 "Erasure" requires each side to erase its ephemeral private key, the chaining key, the handshake cipher key, `k1`, `k2`, the `Split()` cipher states and `prk`, including library copies, on success and on failure or abandonment. A library that keeps uncleared copies does not meet the section. Forward secrecy is now stated as following from that rule.
- F3 (fix, resolved): 3.1 adds a notation line, total sizes (initiation 152, response 96, cookie reply 64 bytes), a response field table, the cookie reply table, index rules, fixed values (`version` 1, reserved and flags zero) with drop rules, `mac1` keyed from the recipient's static key, `mac2` over every byte before it, the cookie `MAC(cookie_secret, src)` with `src` defined for UDP, stream and relay envelope, the under-load rule, the cookie reply encryption (key, random 24-byte nonce with a stated collision bound, `mac1` as associated data), initiator receive rules, and key lifetimes.
- `adr/0043-the-al-handshake-is-fixed-byte-for-byte-on-noise-ikpsk2.md`: renamed from `adr/0043-al-session-keys-come-from-noise-split-outputs-and-the-handshake-hash.md` and extended with the erasure, header binding and cookie decisions.

## Decisions

- N: `mac1` is keyed from the static public key of the message's recipient, so the initiator checks a response with its own key. The old text named only `responder_static`, which was ambiguous for the response.
- N: header `reserved`, payload `reserved`, all flag bits and the response `mac2` are zero in version 1, and a receiver drops a message that breaks this, as it does a wrong `version` or length. Strict rules leave no unauthenticated field with a meaning; new meanings come with a new version.
- N: the cookie is `MAC(cookie_secret, src)`, `cookie_secret` 32 random bytes replaced every 120 seconds, previous secret still accepted, initiator keeps a cookie 120 seconds. `src` is fixed for UDP, stream and relay envelope although only the responder sees it, so it can be tested against vectors.
- N: the cookie reply encrypts the cookie under `BLAKE2s("alloy/al/cookie/v1" || responder_static)` with a random 24-byte nonce and the initiation's `mac1` as associated data, so an off-path party cannot inject a reply and the responder keeps no state per reply (I-06).
- N: header fields stay outside the handshake hash; the spec states it instead of binding them (ADR 0043, alternatives).
- R: ADR 0043 is retitled and its file renamed, since it now records the whole handshake byte layout. It is not on main, so nothing else links to it.

## Issues

- `spec-issue`: `spec/arfc-0003-mesh-wire-protocol.md` section 6 allows "at most one MAC check per packet" before authentication. Under load a responder needs `mac1`, the cookie and `mac2` (three keyed BLAKE2s), plus one AEAD seal for a cookie reply, and this was already implied by the old "must then require a valid `mac2`". Together with the round 1 issue (two DH per initiation, not one), section 6 needs a per-packet cost bound that matches 3.1.
- `follow-up`: the ARFC 0003 conformance suite needs fixed vectors for 3.1 and 3.3: keys, psk, message bytes, `mac1`, a `cookie_secret` and `src` with the cookie and `mac2`, a cookie reply, and `i2r`, `r2i`, `salt`.
- `follow-up`: the task that picks the Noise crate for `alloy-crypto` must show that the crate meets the 3.3 erasure rule (`snow` 0.10.0 zeroizes nothing, per `plan/reports/T-0026.reviewer.1.md`).
- `spec-issue`: ARFC 0003 does not say when an initiator retransmits an initiation or abandons a handshake. 3.1 and 3.3 refer to abandonment without a timeout. Out of scope for T-0026.
