## Summary

Round 2 resolves review 1 F1 to F5 and merges main (alloy-state) into `task/T-0118`.
A `stream` connection stays unproven, within its source's share, until the underlay calls `StreamConn::prove` (F2). When the unproven places are full, a newcomer takes the oldest place of the heaviest source in the heaviest network (IPv4 /24, IPv6 /48), so a flood from few networks closes only its own connections (F3).
A connection due to close refuses every call unread with `Unavailable`, including calls sent before the GOAWAY was read (F4). Each principal holds at most 32 of the 512 API places, and a call counts as in progress for at most 60 s (F5).
The timing rules are tested on the `alloy-sim` clock. The loopback tests no longer assert a scheduling margin (F1).

Findings of review 1:
- F1: the gate's rules run on the sim clock (`conn/tests.rs`). Each loopback assertion now holds whatever the scheduling. The in-progress test holds the call in the store, and the post-`prove` test allows fewer answers.
- F2: `stream` connections are unproven until `StreamConn::prove`. They are closed at `prove` (20 s) or on eviction, and only proven ones take `stream` places.
- F3: eviction by network, then source, then age; a newcomer under its source's share is always placed. The bound and what an attacker can still do are in the `ConnLimits` doc.
- F4: the gate's `closing` flag is set at the bound. Every later call gets `Unavailable` unread (`server.rs` `call`).
- F5: `api_per_principal` = 32. A call stops counting as in progress after `call` (60 s), so a call that never ends holds a place for at most `call` + `idle` + `CLOSE_GRACE`.

## Decisions

- N: Unproven places are given up by eviction, not refusal. The victim is the oldest connection of the heaviest source in the heaviest network (IPv4 /24, IPv6 /48), the newcomer counted.
- N: The port proves a `stream` connection only when the underlay calls `StreamConn::prove` after the AL handshake, and closes it if that has not happened by `prove` (20 s). This changes the `StreamUnderlay` contract in alloy-api, which no implementation outside the tests uses yet.
- N: `ConnLimits::api_per_principal` = 32 of 512, and `ConnLimits::call` = `AdminLimits::call` = 60 s.
- N: A call on a closing connection fails with `Unavailable` and no retry delay. It is not authenticated and not counted as rejected.
- R: `alloy-sim` is a dev-dependency of alloy-api, for clock-exact tests of the gate.

## Issues

- `spec-issue`: ARFC 0008 section 7 should state the API port bounds above. They are now 256/8 unproven with eviction, 512/32 API, 256 `stream`, and 10/20/60/60 s (`spec/arfc-0008-native-api.md`).
- `spec-issue`: ARFC 0004 section 3.6 should say that a `stream` connection must finish its AL handshake within the port's `prove` time (20 s), or the anchor closes it (`spec/arfc-0004-path-manager.md`).
- `follow-up`: A distributed client with one unproven connection in each of 256 networks can still churn other networks' unproven connections. That is DDoS from many networks, stated in the `ConnLimits` doc (`crates/alloy-api/src/transport.rs`).
- `follow-up`: The per-principal share does not bound a tenant with many principals. A per-org share needs the org of a `Principal`, which `Access` does not give (`crates/alloy-api/src/access.rs`).
- `follow-up`: T-0109 re-review F2 (`Transport::of` takes any `RuntimeConfig`) is still open (`crates/alloy-api/src/transport.rs`).
