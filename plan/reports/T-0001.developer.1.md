## Decisions

- Workspace lint set. Rust lints: `missing_docs`, `missing_debug_implementations`, `rust_2018_idioms`, `unreachable_pub`, `unused_qualifications`. Clippy lints: `all`, `allow_attributes_without_reason`, `dbg_macro`, `todo`, `unimplemented`, `undocumented_unsafe_blocks`. Every future crate inherits this set. Reason: it backs the rules in `agent/core/4-evidence.md` and `agent/eng/rust.md` without forcing an allow on every justified `unwrap`.
- `unsafe_code` is not a workspace lint. Each crate states `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` itself, as `agent/eng/rust.md` says. Reason: a workspace-level forbid cannot be relaxed for `alloy-rt`, `alloy-net`, or `alloy-store`.
- `bans.allow` lists workspace members too. cargo-deny applies the list to every crate in the graph, so each new crate, internal or external, must be added to `deny.toml` in the task that creates it.
- The gate runs `clippy`, `test`, and `deny` with `--locked`, and `Cargo.lock` is committed. Reason: reproducible CI. `Cargo.lock` is outside the task's `touches` set, but the gate cannot run without it.
