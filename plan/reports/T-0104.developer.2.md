## Decisions

- N: a previous session's receive key gets its 10 s (3.3) from the first packet under a newer session that shows the peer moved on. Any packet counts, except for an older peer-initiated session against a newer self-initiated one. For that pair, only a non-bind VNI 0 packet counts. Without such a packet the key is kept until its 180 s. This reads "after a rekey" in 3.3 like 3.6 reads it for probe keys.
- N: reassembly bounds count every heap allocation as its size rounded up to 16, plus 16. They count each B-tree element as one fifth of a full node. Under small fragments a peer holds fewer reassemblies, never more memory.
- R: the replay table keeps one entry per static key ever added as a peer, for the life of the link.
