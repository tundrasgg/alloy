## Issues

- `spec-issue`: I agree with the developer: `spec/arfc-0005-simulator-and-natlab.md:662-664` (clause 3.1.1, accepted) names `cargo xtask check-repo` as what rejects operating system calls. After D-0017, the `clippy` step of `cargo xtask ci` rejects them. The clause should name the gate.
- `spec-issue`: `std::collections::HashMap::new()` seeds `RandomState` from operating system randomness, which clause 3.1.1 reserves to the seams. No configured path and no old pattern covers it. The clause or `agent/eng/rust.md` rule 5 should say whether it is allowed.
- `follow-up`: `direct-io` scans only `crates/`, but the Clippy ban applies to every workspace member. A future member outside `crates/` (`tests/sim`, `fuzz/`) could `allow` the lints without a report.
