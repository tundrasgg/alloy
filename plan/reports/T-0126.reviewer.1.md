| F1 | fix | spec/arfc-0002-identity-and-join.md:1190 | The rung 2 login sequence works only in one order, and the text does not say so. | F2 | fix | spec/arfc-0002-identity-and-join.md:1134 | Rung 2 admits a join that names `control` or `anchor`.
