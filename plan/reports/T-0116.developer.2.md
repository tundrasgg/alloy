## Summary

Round 2 resolves review F1. `alloy-link` now follows ARFC 0003 3.4 as written: it binds
nothing for a bind request under a session that no longer qualifies, and seals no bind
there. It drops and counts the request (`DropReason::Bind`). Round 1's deviation and its
crate doc note are gone. As D-0058 expects, a non-zero VNI can now go without a binding
in one direction after a crossed rekey until the next handshake. `tests/faults.rs` asserts
that bound for VNI 5 and keeps the 10 s bound for VNI 0.

## Changes

- `crates/alloy-link/src/link.rs`: `qualifies` (3.4 qualify rule) shared by `choose` and the bind request path; a bind request under a non-qualifying session is dropped before any block is reserved or bind sealed (F1).
- `crates/alloy-link/src/lib.rs`: the "Binds after a crossed rekey" deviation note removed.
- `crates/alloy-link/src/link/tests.rs`: `a_session_that_no_longer_qualifies_binds_nothing`, the 5 s edge on both sides, nothing bound or sealed after it, and the newer session still binds.
- `crates/alloy-link/tests/faults.rs`: after the faults stop, VNI 0 must flow both ways within 10 s. Every VNI must flow both ways within `REKEY_AFTER` + 10 s (130 s).

F1 (block): resolved by following 3.4 as written. T-0119 has not landed, so the code does not wait for it.
