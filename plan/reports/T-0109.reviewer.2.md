## Issues

- `spec-issue`: ARFC 0008 section 7 needs a connection bound, a handshake time, and an idle time for the API port and the local admin socket. It also needs a per-source share, or another rule that stops one client with no credential from holding the port (F1) (`spec/arfc-0008-native-api.md`).
- `follow-up`: I agree with the developer's h2 `Instant::now` follow-up (h2 0.4.20 `src/proto/streams/recv.rs:1049`). Under `mem` it can change the frames sent for a stream reset more than 30 s of wall time earlier. That breaks I-20 only on runs that slow.
