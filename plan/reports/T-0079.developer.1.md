## Summary

`task/T-0079` merges `task/T-0072`, then main (c9b3139). `cargo xtask ci --commits <from>..<to>` runs `check-commits` as a last gate step. A missing, all-zero, or non-ancestor `<from>` falls back to `26eef5f` (D-0027) inside xtask. The workflow now makes one call, `cargo xtask ci`, which resolves F1 of the T-0072 review.

## Changes

- `xtask/src/main.rs`: `ci` accepts `--commits <range>`; `check-repo` rejects it. `ci_steps` appends a `check-commits` step after the six gate steps only when a range is given.
- `xtask/src/main.rs`: `commit_range` rewrites `<from>..<to>` to `26eef5f..<to>` when `<from>` is empty or all zeros, or is not an ancestor of `<to>`. It prints a note when it does this. Other range forms are kept. Usage text documents this. Unit tests cover parsing, step order, and each fallback case.
- `xtask/src/commits.rs`: `Facts.dirs` holds the top-level directories at the commit and its parent (`git ls-tree -d`). `scope_allowed` accepts a scope from `SCOPES`, a crate name, or any of those directories other than `crates`. There is one unit test.
- `xtask/tests/fixtures/commits/scope/pass-top-level-dir/`, `fail-crates-dir/`: a `test(fuzz)` subject passes, and a `feat(crates)` subject fails.
- `clippy.toml`: disallows `std::sync::mpsc::Receiver::recv_timeout`, `std::sync::Condvar::wait_timeout`, `wait_timeout_ms`, and `wait_timeout_while`.
- `xtask/tests/fixtures/clippy/crates/demo/src/lib.rs`, `expected`: `timed_waits` calls all four, and `expected` lists lines 143, 145, 146, and 147. The two `tokio::test` lines move to 153 and 156.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the `Check the commit messages` step is removed. The gate step builds an options array and runs `cargo xtask ci "${options[@]}"` once. On a push, that array holds `--commits "$BEFORE..$GITHUB_SHA"`, plus `--base`/`--branch` as before. Comments are updated.

## Decisions

- `ci --commits` applies the D-0027 fallback inside xtask, not in the workflow. Why: the workflow then runs no `git` command and holds no policy, and the same range gives the same verdict locally and in CI (ADR 0037). `check-commits` alone stays strict.
- A scope may be any top-level directory except `crates`, read from git at the commit and its parent. Why: `agent/eng/git.md` says "any other top-level directory, its name". The checker allowed only a fixed list, so `2db77d4` on main failed and acceptance could not pass. This answers the T-0068 review's second `follow-up`.
- F3's change names "the `Condvar::wait_timeout` family", so the ban covers `wait_timeout_ms` and `wait_timeout_while` as well as `wait_timeout`. `Receiver::recv_deadline` is unstable and is not banned.
