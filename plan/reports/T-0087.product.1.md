## Decisions

- Results go to standard output, everything else (steps, progress, warnings, errors, `Next:`, `Try:`) to standard error (T1). Why: `2>/dev/null` and pipes get only the result, and it extends ARFC 0019 clause 3.1.8 and 3.1.13 consistently.
- The CLI uses only the 16 ANSI foreground colors plus bold and dim, and colors only what needs attention (T9, T11). Why: the user's terminal theme sets shades, and restraint is the direction.
- The console uses system font stacks and downloads no font file (C15). Why: no licensing question under ADR 0035, nothing to fetch (principle 3), and the 300 KiB budget of ARFC 0019 section 7.
- Enum values map mechanically to the glossary word shown in the CLI and console (A5). Why: principle 6 with one rule instead of a per-enum table.
- Where exact compatibility would reveal another organization's resources or weaken ARFC 0020, the safer behavior wins and is listed (X5). Why: `agent/core/1-authority.md` already ranks the security model above product rules; this makes it explicit for compatibility.
