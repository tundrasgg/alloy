## Summary

A node now keeps a probe budget per peer. It seals at most 334 probes to a peer in any 2 seconds, and each kind of probe has its own limit, so no probe set or path MTU search withholds a check. While no probe message between two nodes is late (2 s or more in flight), each seals at most 2,004 probe messages to the other in any 2 seconds. The 2,048-counter probe replay window then rejects no check and no answer to a check. Section 8 names late messages as the one way a probe set can play a part in a check being missed, and it lists delay as a third means for the attacker, next to dropping and overtaking. ADR 0061 is back to its accepted text. An echo of a challenge the node no longer holds is now no echo. The class counts are unchanged and the budget never binds in them. Under the new text, the reviewer's window scenarios no longer fail.

## Decisions

- The fix bounds sealing rate and does not change ARFC 0003. The probe window of 2,048 and one counter per session are ARFC 0003 section 3.6, outside `touches`. A rate bound in ARFC 0004 makes the window reject only late messages, whatever the number of candidate pairs.
- Late means 2 s or more in flight. That is the life of a challenge, so a check or answer that late is missed anyway. A shorter bound would allow more probes, but it would void the guarantee on slow working paths that can still echo.
- Each kind has its own count, so probe sets never compete with checks. The active path's checks and all checks outside rounds have no limit. The rules cap them at 74 in any 2 s, which keeps the 31 s and section 8 end bounds exact for every path, and the 2.4 s bound for the active path. Only round checks of paths that are not active can be withheld, and only with 7 or more such paths.
- Pairs beyond the budget are probed round robin across rounds rather than dropped. Every pair gets probed, and the 5 s bound now states its condition: at most 18 pairs per probe set.
- I did not lower the 1,024 probe challenge bound to 200. It now has headroom, and changing it would change a table bound that no finding names.
