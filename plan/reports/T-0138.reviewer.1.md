## Issues

- `spec-issue`: ARFC 0004 section 7 (row 1) and section 3.2 step 6 replace the least recently verified peer-reflexive candidate at the bound, even one that is the far endpoint of the active direct path. Step 9 and section 8 ("Moves need a fresh echo", "Start at a node") say an active direct path is left only when its far endpoint fails. The two disagree, and an underlay attacker that carries 64 exchanges can use the gap to move a node off a working path at once. `spec/arfc-0004-path-manager.md`.
- `spec-issue`: ARFC 0004 section 3.1.2 sends a reflection request "when its local candidates change". It does not say whether a change of the reflexive or mapped candidate counts. `spec/arfc-0004-path-manager.md`.
