## Summary

Reviewed `task/T-0117` at 9904bc5. `cargo xtask ci` passes and the diff stays inside `touches` plus its report.
One `block`. Clause 3.1.15 builds a Windows `Private` entry from a descriptor holding an empty, unprotected list. On NTFS that gives a protected, empty list, not the parent's entries. So `alloyd` would be locked out of every file it creates, and its host file breaks ARFC 0002 3.12.
Five `fix` findings: a missing ADR, the Linux mode rule, `set_mode` durability, the route from `native`'s `dir` to an `RtPath`, and two gaps in how 3.12 reads facts.
