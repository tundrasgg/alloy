## Summary

The core pace rule, the task protocol, and the lead role now carry D-0028's lean direction.
A `build` or `verify` task against an interface already merged on main targets 2,000 changed lines; every other task stays at 400.
Review is required for `build` and `interface` tasks and for security-relevant `spec` or `process` behavior changes. Clarifications, research, and small lead-checked tooling builds are exempt.
Non-`block` `fix` and `note` findings are recorded on the board and folded into the next task on the same files.

## Changes

- `agent/core/6-pace.md`: "Small tasks" adds the 2,000-line target. "One review pass" now states which tasks get a review and which do not, and adds folding of non-`block` findings.
- `agent/protocol/tasks.md`: lifecycle steps 4 and 5 point to new `Review` and `Folding findings` sections. The non-build skip line now follows the Review rule. Sizing matches the new targets.
- `agent/roles/lead.md`: the loop records non-`block` findings on the board. Cutting tasks gains a sizing line and a folding line. A new `Review` section says the lead dispatches the reviewer only where the rule requires one, and runs acceptance itself for an exempt tooling build.

## Decisions

- A `verify` task is reviewed together with its paired build task on the build branch, as lifecycle step 4 already did. The task scope did not name `verify`.
- Any task the scope neither requires nor exempts, for example `product` or a non-security `spec` or `process` behavior change, merges without review. This follows D-0028: "review only code and security-relevant behavior changes".
- A recorded finding on the board carries its report path, its finding id, and the files its `where` names. The lead needs those three to match it to the `touches` of a later task.
