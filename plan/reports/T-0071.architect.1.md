## Decisions

- N: ADR 0060 does not supersede ADR 0035. ADR 0035 allows "similar permissive terms", and NCSA is a permissive BSD-style license, so 0060 narrows the stricter list in `agent/eng/deps.md` for one crate rather than reversing 0035.
- N: the ADR also lists the `licenses.clarify` restatement (option 3 of the T-0061 round 1 question) as rejected, since the pilot chose option 1 over it; the task named only the other two alternatives.
- R: the ADR states the fuzz package under `fuzz/` is outside the root workspace, which is the layout T-0061 round 1 built (`plan/reports/T-0061.developer.1.md`); `fuzz/` is not yet on main.

## Issues

- `follow-up`: once T-0061 round 2 puts `libfuzzer-sys` on `bans.allow` and adds its license exception in `deny.toml`, nothing mechanical stops a crate in the root workspace from depending on it, which ADR 0060 forbids. A check in `cargo xtask ci` that the root `Cargo.lock` holds no `libfuzzer-sys` would enforce it (`xtask/src/`).
