`SleepUntil::poll` (`os/mod.rs:243-252`) reads `is_shut()` and then polls the `tokio` sleep. `shutdown` sets the flag and then stops the driver. A poll that reads the flag before the store and reaches `poll_elapsed` after the driver stops hits that assert. One way to hit it: a thread in `OsHost::block_on(... sleep ...)` while another thread calls `shutdown`.
