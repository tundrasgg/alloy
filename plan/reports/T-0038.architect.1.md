## Summary

ARFC 0003 section 3.6 now defines probe messages byte for byte: a 56-byte header (sender `node_id`, epoch, index, nonce), a 56-byte body (flags, challenge, echo, observed endpoint), and padding. Unpadded messages are 128 bytes, and answers are never padded. Every probe message carries an epoch, drawn from a persistent per-node counter and renewed at each probe set, and a per-epoch index. Receivers drop older epochs and repeated indexes with a 2,048-index window per peer, kept at least 10 s after last use (ADR 0046). The static-static probe key is derived once per probe record, never per packet. Section 6 gains a probe row: no DH with a record, one DH without one, none under load.

Length arithmetic: header 4 + 16 + 8 + 4 + 24 = 56. Body 4 + 16 + 16 + 20 = 56, plus the 16-byte tag. Total 128.

Why replays cannot evict challenges (F6 of `plan/reports/T-0031.reviewer.1.md`): a node issues an answer challenge only while it is probing the sender, and only in reply to a probe (ARFC 0004 section 3.2 step 5). Probes are sent only inside probe sets, and each set starts a new epoch. Once the receiver accepts any message of the sender's newest set, every older epoch fails the replay check. Within that epoch, each index passes at most once while the record is held. A probing node sends every 200 ms and probes for 1 s past its last round, so it keeps the record for its whole probing period (10 s rule). The residual is stated in 3.6: after a record has been idle 10 s and was dropped, a message can pass once more, but only until a newer epoch arrives. ARFC 0004 section 8 bounds what it draws.

## Decisions

- N: replay protection is a persistent epoch per probe set plus a per-peer index window (ADR 0046). A per-message counter would leave earlier probe sets inside the window. Session-bound probes would make ARFC 0004 depend on a session before probing. Waiting for a fresh answer before answering would keep the mixed cases of ARFC 0004 section 3.3 on the relay.
- N: the probe key is `HKDF(salt = lo || hi, IKM = X25519 || psk)` with label `alloy/al/probe/v1`. It binds both static keys and the pair's pre-shared key and seals both directions, with random 24-byte nonces as the cookie reply uses. It is computed once per record. Random nonces were chosen over nonces derived from epoch and index so that a restored counter cannot cause nonce reuse.
- N: every answer carries an observed endpoint (family, port, address). ARFC 0004 section 3.1.1 requires anchors to report the observed source, and no other field could carry it. Answers to messages that arrived in a relay envelope report family 0.
- N: probe records are bounded by the node's active session limit (ARFC 0004 section 7) and kept at least 10 s after last use. At the bound, with every record still held, a node drops probe messages from peers it has no record for and sends no probes to them (I-21).
- N: `sender node_id` and the replay fields are associated data, and a message naming the receiver as sender is dropped before any cryptographic operation, so a message cannot be reflected back to its sender.
- R: the Decisions line in the ARFC 0003 header now lists 0046. It is a cross-reference outside sections 3.6 and 6.

## Issues

- `spec-issue`: `spec/arfc-0004-path-manager.md` section 8 ("Probes carry no counter ... not a replay check") and ADR 0039's consequence line no longer hold after this task. T-0043 or a follow-up should restate section 8 against ARFC 0003 section 3.6.
- `spec-issue`: `spec/arfc-0003-mesh-wire-protocol.md` section 3.1 computes a cookie from "the `src` of the envelope" for relayed initiations, but the relay envelope in section 3.7 has no `src` field. This existed before this task. For that reason section 3.6 reports observed family 0 for relayed messages.
- `spec-issue`: `spec/arfc-0004-path-manager.md` section 3.5 says relayed paths use 1,280 "until probed" but never says whether path MTU probes travel inside relay envelopes. Section 3.6 sizes them for both cases.
- `follow-up`: `spec/arfc-0003-mesh-wire-protocol.md` section 7 defines "under load" by pending handshakes only. A flood of probe messages that name real node_ids costs one DH each and never counts as load. That cost is bounded per packet and allowed by section 6, but it is not throttled.
- `follow-up`: the ARFC 0003 conformance suite needs tests for 3.6. They should cover the byte layout and fixed value drops, the replay check (older epoch, repeated index, the 2,048 window), the epoch counter across a crash, the 10 s record keep, the record bound, and the section 6 probe row (DH count with and without a record, and under load).
