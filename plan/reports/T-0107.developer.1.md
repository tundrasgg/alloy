## Summary

`alloy-state` holds the `log-store` and `state-store` seams of ARFC 0006 5.1, their `mem` and `native` (`redb` 4.5.0) implementations with the 5.2 configurations, the 4.1 export, and one `openraft` 0.10.0 group for one voter on an `AsyncRuntime` over `alloy-rt`, with the snapshots and purge of 3.10 and 3.12.4.
Resources (2.1) have create, get, list, update with a generation (3.5), and two-phase delete (3.6), applied as group commands.
Every acceptance command passes. The change is 4,742 lines without `Cargo.lock`, against a 2,000-line target, so per the task notes the Node kind fields (2.6), the status messages of 4.3 as soft state, and node state (3.15) are cut and not built.

## Decisions

- N: `NativeStoreConfig` and `MemStoreConfig` are public, beyond the 5.1 listing, so `alloyd` and the suites can write bindings.
- N: `openraft`'s `AsyncRuntime` has only associated functions, so the runtime is found through a per-poll thread-local scope that `Group` enters around every call into `openraft`; spawned tasks inherit it. The seed replay test shows no timer, task, or draw escapes it.
- N: `from_value` accepts empty `dir` and `name` (defaults, ARFC 0001 clause 3.13 round trip); the factories reject them with `Config(Invalid)` naming the field.
- N: a resource's `generation` rises on every accepted write; `delete` sets `deleting`, `remove` removes a `deleting` resource. Values use this crate's encoding, since `state.redb` contents are not a stable format (4.2).
- R: a malformed export is reported by `finish`, never by `write`; Raft node ids are the 16 `node_id` bytes as `u128`, `LeaderId` of `leader_id_adv`.
- R: `open` waits until the log present at open is applied and a leader is known (3.12.3), at most 60 s.

## Issues

- spec-issue: ARFC 0006 5.2 wants `native` files at mode 0600 on Linux; `redb::Database::create` uses the umask, and `clippy.toml` forbids `std::fs::OpenOptions` and `set_permissions` outside `alloy-rt`. Files are not 0600 today (`crates/alloy-state/src/native.rs`).
- follow-up: the Node kind fields of 2.6, `NodeStatus` checks and soft state of 4.3 and 3.14, and node state of 3.15 in `crates/alloy-state`.
- follow-up: `openraft` reads `SystemTime::now` when it formats an instant for display (`openraft-0.10.0/src/display_ext/display_instant.rs:45`), outside the runtime; it changes no behavior, and replay is unaffected.
- follow-up: `sha2` 0.10 enters through `quorum-set` in the `openraft` tree; no Alloy crate uses it (`agent/eng/crypto.md` rule 1 is kept).
