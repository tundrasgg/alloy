## Summary

`cargo xtask check-commits <rev-range>` walks the range along the first parent, oldest first, and prints `<commit id>: <rule>: <message>` for each breach of twelve rules, with one passing and one failing fixture per rule.
`cargo test -p xtask` and `cargo xtask ci` exit 0.
`cargo xtask check-commits 40c70e9..main` exits 1, and it is right to: three commits on main made after the format was adopted have subjects over 72 characters (5470428 at 73, 5c17993 at 76, 78d3175 at 73). The fallback range in the task notes, `2102651..main`, fails on the same three. `2102651` (`docs(adr)`) passes, because no rule compares the type with the content.
`78d3175..main` exits 0. Blocked on which range, or which mechanism, counts for acceptance (Question).

## Changes

- `xtask/src/commits.rs`: new. Message parser, the twelve rules as a pure `check(message, facts)`, `check_range` that reads only the facts each commit needs from git, unit tests, and the fixture tests.
- `xtask/src/main.rs`: `check-commits <rev-range>` task and usage text; the range must be one argument that is not empty and does not start with `-`.
- `xtask/src/rules/path_lock.rs`, `xtask/src/rules/mod.rs`: `is_locked`, `task_type`, `is_task_id` shared with `check-commits`. `check-repo` behaves the same.
- `xtask/src/text.rs`, `xtask/src/rules/code.rs`: the manifest `[package]` name reader moved to `text::package_name` so both commands use it.
- `xtask/src/tree.rs`: `file_at` moved here from `main.rs`.
- `xtask/tests/fixtures/commits/<rule>/{pass,fail}/`: `message`, and where the rule needs them, `crates`, `changed`, and `parent/plan/tasks/`.

Rules: `subject-format`, `subject-length` (characters, not bytes), `type`, `scope` (the fixed list from `agent/eng/git.md`, `ci`, `repo`, and the package names of `crates/*/Cargo.toml` in the commit and its first parent), `trailing-period`, `blank-line`, `footers` (each footer a `Key: value` line in the final paragraph, at most once, in the order Task, Spec, BREAKING CHANGE; an allowed footer in the body is reported), `trailers` (any other key in the footers, and any hyphenated trailer such as `Co-Authored-By:` or `Signed-off-by:` anywhere in the body), `task-id`, `task-required` (all types except `chore(plan)`), `breaking` (`!` exactly when `BREAKING CHANGE:`), and `path-lock` (D-0016: a commit that changes a path locked in `agent/eng/testing.md` fails unless a task in its `Task:` footer has `type: verify` in `plan/tasks/` at the commit's first parent).

## Decisions

- Scopes from crates are the `[package]` names of `crates/<dir>/Cargo.toml`, or `<dir>` when there is no name, in the commit and its first parent, read only when the scope is not on the fixed list.
- The footers are the last paragraph when it opens with a `Key: value` line. Hyphenated trailers in the body are reported too, so a trailer cannot hide above a body line.
- Not checked, because they need judgment or are outside the task's rule list: a lowercase description, a body of two to five lines, `Spec:` matching the task's `spec` field, and the type matching the content.
- The diff is about 1,000 lines, over the 400 line target. Most of it is tests and the 43 fixture files.
