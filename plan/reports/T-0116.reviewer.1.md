All 6 losses are one VNI 5 packet each. Each one follows a forced crossed rekey that a
second rekey made less than 1 s earlier. Each is the session limit of 3.4 (rule 3 of 3.3),
not rule 2. The node holds one more session than the peer: an unconfirmed responder
session left by the faults. It discards its oldest session, which the peer still seals
VNI 5 under for up to 5 s. The code does what the spec says, so this is filed as an issue,
not a finding.
