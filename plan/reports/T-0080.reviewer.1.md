Model of the failure rule. It lives in scratch and is not committed. `failrule.py` is about 110 lines. It applies, as written on the branch, step 6 "fails" (`:123-124`), the checks of section 3.4 (`:230-237`: 25 s, a retry when the latest challenge is 2 s unechoed and the node is not probing, one per probe set round), the probe set of step 3 (`:91-96`: one probe per candidate pair per round, 200 ms apart, probing until 1 s after round 5), and padded probes (section 3.5). It models node X's view of the far endpoint E of its active direct path, with a 100 ms round trip. At the instant when the fail rule and the retry rule are both due, the default order is fail first. That is the order the ARFC's own figures imply (31 s at `:245`, 2.8 s at `:247`).

```
$ python -I failrule.py
baseline: 1 pair, probe set at 10 s, no loss                             E fails at: None
4 pairs to E, answered pair sent first in each round, probe set at 10 s  E fails at: 12.8
4 pairs to E, answered pair sent last in each round, probe set at 10 s   E fails at: None
3 pairs to E (answered first), probe set at 10 s, then 1 check lost      E fails at: 14.8
1 pair, 3 padded probes over the path MTU 1 s apart from 30 s (RFC 8899 PROBE_TIMER=1 s) E fails at: 34.0
1 pair, same 3 padded probes, tie order retry-first                      E fails at: None
1 pair, E stops answering at 5 s, tie order fail-first                   E fails at: 31.0
1 pair, E stops answering at 5 s, tie order retry-first                  E fails at: None
```

The "4 pairs" rows are a node with 4 own candidates on distinct bases paired with the advertised E, where only one pair reaches E. An example is a second interface with no route to E. E answers every probe on the working pair, and nothing is lost. The ARFC fixes no send order within a round, so both rows conform.

RFC 8899 (https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8899.html): section 5.1.1 says PROBE_TIMER "MUST NOT be smaller than 1 second and SHOULD be larger than 15 seconds". Section 5.1.2 says "The default value of MAX_PROBES is 3". With PROBE_TIMER under 2 s, the three probes of a size above the path MTU are the three most recent challenges to E. That is the normal end of a search "up to the interface MTU" (`:287`).
