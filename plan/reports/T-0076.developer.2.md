## Decisions

- Class N: no simulator lock is held while a waker that came from task code is woken, cloned, or dropped. A sleep clones the waker before it locks `core`. Fired wakers are woken in `Shared::settle`, before the other deferred work, in firing order. The executor's own task wakers only touch the ready queue, so they are still made and dropped under `core`. This is the lock rule that T-0077 and T-0094 must follow for sockets and disk futures.
- Class N: a history's identity is the allocation of an `Arc<()>` token, compared with `Arc::ptr_eq`. This needs no global counter (`agent/eng/rust.md`, no global mutable state), and nothing observable depends on an address, only on identity.
- Routine: at most one `StallEnd` event is pending per host, at the latest end of the stalls in force. A shorter stall adds no event, as in round 1.
