## Decisions

- Issue 1, mixed pairs (behavior change, ADR 0039): an authenticated probe is answered at its source, one reply no larger than the probe. An unknown source becomes a peer-reflexive candidate that is probed for 5 rounds and counts toward the 64-candidate limit. A round trip counts only when the reply comes from the endpoint the probe was sent to. As a result, both sides endpoint-dependent stays relayed for every filtering value, not only for the presets. R-0003 option A is adopted, so rows 1 and 2 against columns 5, 7, 8 and 10 are `direct`.
- Issue 2, timeout range: widened to 10 s to 600 s. Out-of-range values are rejected when the world is built, never clamped. Reason: silent clamping would hide a wrong preset. No ADR, because this is a lab parameter range and does not constrain later design.
- Issue 2, related lab rules: one external address per family (RFC 6888 REQ-2), a separate timer for each mapping and each filter entry, and refresh on outbound packets only. ARFC 0005 was silent on these, and the section 3.3 rule and the keepalive expectation need them.
- Issue 3, keepalive: stays 25 s and is now explicitly per side. The `short-timeout` preset (port-restricted behavior with a 20 s timeout) exists to exercise the failure case. Its expectations hold for every seed: each node either keeps receiving on the path within 75 s or marks it failed within 75 s; packets sent after a failure mark are delivered; loss before that is allowed. An exact sequence of events is not specified, because it depends on keepalive phase and on other traffic sharing the mapping, which differ by seed.
- Issue 4, `udp-blocked`: kept the 3 s UDP wait and gave nodes reachable only over `stream` their own bound of 5 s to relay reachability. The 3 s now counts from the node's first UDP packet to an anchor. No ADR: node behavior is unchanged, and the old 2 s bound could not be met by the mechanism the spec already required.
