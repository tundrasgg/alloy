## Summary

ARFC 0007 now states the one way to tenant data outside the API: a cloud admin who controls a node, a local admin always included, can put an org's guests and volumes on that node and read them there. It also states what records that way and why it is consistent with 3.3, 3.2, and I-02 (F1 of `plan/reports/T-0052.reviewer.2.md`).
Clause 3.3 and section 8 now limit their claims to access through the API. The "there is no third way" sentence is gone.
Clause 3.10.7 now names 3.2's default deny as one of the two things that can still refuse the recovered key, for a target inside an org other than `O`, and says why a cluster role never lifts it (F2).
This is a clarification with no behavior change, so there is no ADR. ADR 0055 is not edited, and ARFC 0007 governs where they differ.

## Decisions

- F1 is resolved by stating the path, not by bounding it. Bounding it, for example with a record in an org's chain whenever its guests or volumes are placed on a node that a local admin changed, would be a behavior change that needs an ADR. The task offered either, and ARFC 0020 section 8 already leaves this case undefended in version 1.
- F2: 3.2 was right and 3.10.7 over-promised. A cluster role is not "a policy in the target's org", so by 3.2's text a `cloud-admin` call on a target inside another org is refused unless that org allows it. 3.10.7 now says so instead of changing 3.2. This matches 3.3, where cloud admins manage cluster-scope targets.

## Issues

- `spec-issue`: ARFC 0020 section 8 lists "a malicious cloud admin with access to a node's hardware" as undefended, but it does not name user id 0 on a node, or steering placement onto a node, which ARFC 0007 clause 3.3 now relies on (`spec/arfc-0020-security-model.md`).
- `follow-up`: ADR 0055's ruling on I-02 ("two ways only") and its Consequences line ("only by the two ways") miss the way through a node. ARFC 0007 section 8 governs under `agent/core/1-authority.md`. A later ADR or ADR 0055's successor should restate it (`adr/0055-root-on-a-control-node-is-a-full-break-glass-administrator.md`).
- `follow-up`: the pending I-02 change in `agent/domain/invariants.md` (from T-0052) should also cover the way through a node: I-02 binds principals through calls, and reads on a trusted node are outside it.
- `follow-up`: whether a policy in an org can allow a principal of another org at all is unclear, since a statement (2.4) has no principal field. That decides whether 3.10.7's first refusal can ever be lifted for a target in another org (`spec/arfc-0007-tenancy-and-iam.md` clauses 2.4 and 3.2). This is the reviewer's 3.2 issue from T-0052 round 2, narrowed.
