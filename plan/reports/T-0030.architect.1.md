## Summary

ARFC 0003 section 6 now limits the work done on a packet before it is authenticated, per message type, in DH operations and MAC and AEAD operations. The limits match what `IKpsk2` needs: two DH before an initiation or a response is authenticated, none for an initiation under load without a valid `mac2`, and four per side for a whole handshake.
Section 3.1 now states the cookie guarantee that holds: at least 120 seconds from creation on the responder's clock. An initiation sent within one round trip of the end of the initiator's keep window may draw a fresh cookie reply. This resolves F1 of `plan/reports/T-0026.reviewer.2.md`.
Both edits are clarifications, so there is no ADR. ADR 0011 already chose Noise IK, so the old "one DH per packet" could never be met by the handshake 3.1 requires. The cookie rotation and keep window rules are unchanged.

## Changes

- `spec/arfc-0003-mesh-wire-protocol.md`: section 6 replaces "one MAC check, or one DH when not under load" with a cost table per message type. Section 3.1 adds a "Cookie validity" paragraph and says a new cookie replaces the old one for that responder.

Cookie arithmetic behind the new 3.1 text: secret `i` is current on `[120i, 120(i+1))` and accepted as previous until `120(i+2)`. A cookie made at `t` in the first interval therefore stays valid until at least `t + 120`, with equality as `t` approaches `120(i+1)`. The initiator keeps it until `t + d1 + 120`, so an initiation sent at the end of that window reaches the responder at `t + d1 + d2 + 120`. That is up to one round trip `d1 + d2` past the guarantee. This matches the reviewer's probe (seed 91: made 719.9105, arrives 840.0821, secret expires 840).

## Decisions

- N: section 6 is a clarification, not a behavior change. ADR 0011 (Noise IK) and ADR 0043 (`IKpsk2`) require two DH per handshake message, so the old limit contradicted accepted text from the start. The table keeps the protection the old rule meant: bounded cost per packet, no DH under load without a valid `mac2`, and response DH only for initiations the receiver has pending. Types other than 1 to 3 keep the old rule word for word.
- N: section 6 also resolves the reviewer's confirmed `spec-issue` (T-0026 reviewer round 2): "at most one MAC check per packet" conflicted with the `mac1`, two `mac2` and cookie reply seal that 3.1 requires under load. The table now lists those operations. The conflict was in the same sentence, so the fix stays inside the task's `touches` and `spec` fields.
- N: for F1, I took the reviewer's third option and stated the guarantee that holds, rather than shortening the initiator's keep window or lengthening the responder's acceptance. Either of those changes a required timer, which would need an ADR under the task's scope. When the guarantee is missed, the cost is one extra round trip and one stateless cookie reply.
- R: "keeps the cookie for that responder" now says the new cookie replaces any held one. This was the only consistent reading of one cookie per responder.

## Issues

- `follow-up`: the ARFC 0003 conformance suite needs tests for the section 6 table. They should count X25519, MAC and AEAD operations per packet through the `alloy-crypto` interface on the simulator. They also need the 3.1 cookie validity rule: accepted for initiations that arrive under 120 seconds after creation, across a secret rotation. `spec/arfc-0003-mesh-wire-protocol.md` sections 3.1 and 6.
- `spec-issue`: section 3.6 does not say whether a probe's static-static key is computed per packet or once per peer. Section 6 keeps the old "one DH when not under load" for probes, so under load a receiver can process a probe only with a key it computed earlier. Section 3.6 should state this. `spec/arfc-0003-mesh-wire-protocol.md:286`.
