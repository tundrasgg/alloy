## Summary

Resolved F1 of `plan/reports/T-0127.reviewer.1.md`. The link now records every key passed to `add_peer`, and `note_transient` never puts such a key in `transient`. Its replay floor therefore survives `remove_peer`, unknown re-initiations and any flood past `UNKNOWN_REPLAY_ENTRIES` (ARFC 0003 section 3.2).
Resolved F2: `wipe` and `source_address` each carry only their own doc comment.
The reviewer's attack scenario is now a committed unit test. It fails without the fix (`left: Ok(()) right: Err(Replay)`) and passes with it.

## Changes

- `crates/alloy-link/src/link.rs`: new `added: BTreeSet<PublicKey>` field, filled by `add_peer`. `note_transient` returns early for keys in `added`. The `set_accept_unknown` doc now says a key once added keeps its entry for good. The `wipe` doc comment is back on `wipe` (F2).
- `crates/alloy-link/src/link/tests.rs`: `a_key_once_a_peer_keeps_its_replay_entry`. K is a peer and its initiation is accepted. K is removed, then initiates again and is accepted as unknown. Next, 65,537 fresh keys are recorded the way `on_initiation` records them. Finally, replaying both of K's initiations gives `Err(Replay)`.

## Decisions

- The regression test is a unit test, not an integration test. It floods `transient` through `highest.insert` plus `note_transient`, the same two steps `on_initiation` runs for an unknown accept. 65,537 real handshakes would take minutes in a debug build. K's two initiations and the replays still go through `Link::receive`.

## Issues

- `spec-issue`: unchanged from T-0127. Keys never added as peers still lose their replay entry after `UNKNOWN_REPLAY_ENTRIES` newer unknown keys, so ARFC 0003 section 3.2 needs a stated bound (`crates/alloy-link/src/link.rs`, `note_transient`).
