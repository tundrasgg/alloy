These attack readings were tried:
- A hostile anchor rewrites a carried `join_request`, moves it to another mesh key, or sends the joiner's `identity_pub` with its own token. Each breaks `identity_signature` or `token_signature`. No finding.
- A hostile anchor replays a carried `join_request` unchanged after the joiner withdraws, after `RejectNode`, after a cloud admin deletes the node, and after a section 6 expiry deletion. Each passes checks 1 to 6 while the token is usable, and with `join_approval` `off` (the default) it admits a node and issues a record (F1).
- Signed message prefixes: `alloy/id/join/v1` (16 bytes), `alloy/id/withdraw/v1`, `alloy/id/leave/v1`, and `alloy-renew-v1` differ within their first 9 bytes, and `alloy/id/token-key/v1` is 21 bytes. No message of one kind is a message of another. No finding.
- Kind bound: `join_state` every 2 s during a 10 s `withdraw`, repeated `alloy node leave`, and a heartbeat behind a `renew`. Each merges into the held request of its kind. No finding.
