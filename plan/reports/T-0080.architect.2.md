## Summary

A far endpoint now fails only on its own path's checks. A check is an unpadded probe sent from the path's near endpoint, the local candidate whose challenge verified that endpoint. Probes on other candidate pairs and padded probes no longer count. The fail rule and the retry rule are stated so that the order of rules at one instant does not matter. Section 8 and ADR 0061 now say that overtaking, with no packet dropped, is enough to make three checks miss. Section 3.6 says how an anchor sends to a node homed over `stream`. A new model reproduces 34 of 81 and 872 of 7,290 with 0 mismatches, and the 3.25 s worst case under the 5 s bound.

How each finding of `plan/reports/T-0080.reviewer.1.md` is resolved:

- F1 (block): resolved. Only checks count toward failure: unpadded probes from the path's near endpoint to E while the path is usable (3.4). Probes from other local candidates and padded probes are excluded by name. So a probe set over 4 pairs, one lost check, and a PMTU search all leave a working path up (model, `scen.py`). An attacker who starts a probe set no longer fails the path by doing so. Section 8 states this. The class results and the 5 s bound hold under the new rule (model below).
- F2 (fix): resolved. A check is missed 2 s after it was sent if it was not echoed. E fails at that miss when the two checks sent just before it were also missed and no later check was echoed. The retry is sent only when E does not fail, no check is outstanding, and the node is not probing. Both conditions read only checks sent earlier, and the ARFC says so. The model gives the same fail time under both orders: 30.9 s after the last echo of a check.
- F3 (fix): resolved. Section 8 now says the attacker must keep the echo of each of three consecutive checks from being accepted from E. Dropping does that, and so does overtaking with a copy from another source, which uses the challenge up and turns the genuine answer into a replay. It says overtaking drops no packet. The time is more than 2 s: 2.4 s in one probe set and 6 s outside one. ADR 0061 no longer says "blocking".
- F4 (fix): resolved. Section 3.6 states the rule for `stream` homes, and 3.1.2 points to it. The stale cross-reference to "section 3.2 step 6" is gone.

## Decisions

- Failure is per path, read from that path's own checks, not per far endpoint over every challenge sent to it. This adds the near endpoint, so a check runs on the same 5-tuple as the session traffic it protects, and the check keepalive refreshes that NAT state.
- The retry is sent only when no check of the path is outstanding. This replaces "when the latest challenge goes 2 s unechoed" and removes the tie that F2 found.
- Three consecutive misses are still the fail threshold. Two overlapping probe sets can put three checks within about 200 ms, so section 8 states the minimum as "more than 2 seconds", not 2.4 s.
- An anchor sends to a `stream` home only on its connection, with no echo and no lapse. A TCP connection the node opened has no endpoint that a UDP packet could move.
