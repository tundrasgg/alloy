## Summary

`alloy-seam` holds the whole ARFC 0001 section 2.1 interface: names, `Detail`, `ConfigValue` and its section 4 codec, `SeamConfig`, `Binding`, the registry (clauses 3.11 and 3.12), and `Slot` with the live swap protocol (clauses 3.14 to 3.16). The crate has 33 unit tests.
`alloy-rt` holds ARFC 0005 sections 2.1 to 2.5: the five seam traits, every value type with its validation, the seam markers, `RuntimeConfig`, `Runtime` (clause 3.1.14), `JoinHandle`, and `Elapsed`. The crate has 20 unit tests.
Section 2.6 (`OsHost`, `register_os`) is left out, because the task puts the `os` implementations out of scope.
Neither crate has `unsafe`. Both carry `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`. Main is merged into the branch and the gate passes on the merge.

## Decisions

- N: `thiserror` 2.0.21 is added to the `deny.toml` allowlist with its build-time tree: `thiserror-impl`, `proc-macro2`, `quote`, `syn` 3.0.10, and `unicode-ident`. ARFC 0001 and 0005 require `thiserror` derives, and `agent/eng/deps.md` lists it as a standing choice, so no research note was written. `cargo deny` passes the licenses.
- N: `Kind` and `ImplName` implement `Display`, which writes `as_str()`. Section 2.1 does not list this impl, but its error attributes (`"{kind}/{name}"`) need it.
- R: `Registry::instantiate` returns `BuildError::Failed` when a marker's `KIND` is not a valid `Kind`, because `SeamUnavailable` cannot hold an invalid kind. No factory is called, and such a marker can never be registered.
- R: `RegistryBuilder::register` checks in the order clause 3.11 lists: `InvalidKind`, `KindConflict`, `Duplicate`, `Full`. `Kind::new` reports `TooLong` before `InvalidByte`.
- R: `RtPath::join` rejects a component that holds `/`, so it always adds exactly one component.
- R: `Runtime::timeout` fixes its deadline when it is called, not at its first poll. `Runtime::sleep` does the same.
- R: `IoBuf` gets its 4,096-byte alignment without `unsafe`. It over-allocates by `ALIGN - 1` bytes and offsets into the allocation, so each non-empty buffer carries up to 4,095 extra bytes. An aligned allocator is a contained change if a benchmark asks for one.
- R: `alloy-rt` carries `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` for now. The `os` task relaxes it when it needs `unsafe`.
- R: `Slot`'s `Debug` output is written by hand, with no `S: Debug` bound, and shows the kind, the binding, the generation, and the instance.

## Issues

- `spec-issue`: `spec/arfc-0001-runtime-seams.md` section 2.1 omits the `Display` impls of `Kind` and `ImplName` that its own error messages need. It is also silent on what `instantiate` returns for an invalid `S::KIND`.
- `spec-issue`: `spec/arfc-0001-runtime-seams.md` clauses 3.10 and 3.11 do not say whether their lists set a precedence (`TooLong` against `InvalidByte`, and `KindConflict` against `Duplicate` against `Full`). The conformance suite can only pin the order once the spec states one.
- `spec-issue`: `spec/arfc-0005-simulator-and-natlab.md` clause 3.1.14 does not say when the timer of `timeout` starts (at the call or at the first poll). It also does not say whether `RtPath::join` accepts a component that holds `/`.
- `follow-up`: `plan/STATE.md` says T-0007 adds `blake3` to `deny.toml`, but `plan/tasks/T-0007.md` does not ask for it and no crate here needs it, so it was not done.
- `process-issue`: the task is about 4,550 changed lines, about 4,470 of them code, docs, and unit tests in the two crates. The fixed interface in ARFC 0001 and 0005 sets that size, and a split by crate would have kept each piece nearer the 400-line target in `agent/core/6-pace.md`.
