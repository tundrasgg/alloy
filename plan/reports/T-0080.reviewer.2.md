Round 1 F2, F3 and F4 are resolved. F1 is resolved for probes on other pairs and for padded probes, but checks share the 1,024-entry probe challenge table. A probe set over more than 1,024 candidate pairs, which the 64-candidate bound allows, evicts the checks' challenges. In B, with 1,025 or more pairs, the 1,024 probes sent after a check in the same round evict its challenge before the echo arrives. That happens in every round, so the last round's check is evicted too and the rescue clause does not save the path. Below 1,025 pairs the rescue clause saves it: the last round's check survives and is echoed before the third miss. That is why the 400-pair and 110-pair rows do not fail. Section 7 allows 64 candidates of a node and 64 per peer (`:364`), so up to 4,096 pairs. Section 8 item 1 allows "one per candidate pair per round" (`:394-397`). No other spec bounds the candidate count (`grep` over `spec/` for candidate limits finds only `:135` and `:413`).
