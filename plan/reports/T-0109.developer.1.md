## Summary

`proto/alloy/` holds the error codes and the `cluster` v1alpha1 service (`GetNode`, `ListNodes`), every item documented; `alloy-proto` compiles them with `protox` + `tonic-prost-build` (no `protoc`, no network) and fails the build on any undocumented item (checked by removing three comments: build failed naming all three).
`alloy-api` serves gRPC over `hyper` HTTP/2 on `alloy-rt` streams and tasks: the API port (`rustls` + `aws-lc-rs`, TLS 1.3 only, ALPN `h2`/`alloy-al/1` chosen from the ClientHello, `no_application_protocol` otherwise, hello without ALPN included), and the local admin socket (h2 prior knowledge, local admin principal).
Pagination, the error trailer `alloy-error-bin`, bearer-token authentication, `UNIMPLEMENTED` for unknown paths, 4 MiB request limit. Store and access are trait arguments (`NodeStore`, `Access`).
The client connects with a pinned `api_ca_fp` and iPAddress check, sends the token, and offers `list_all_nodes` and `retry` (A18).
Size: about 3,300 new lines excluding `Cargo.lock` (520 of them tests), over the 2,000 target.

## Decisions

- `rustls`, `tokio-rustls`, `webpki` sit in alloy-api, as the task directs. `alloy-crypto` is not in touches, and T-0106 holds it. See issue 1.
- tonic has only `codegen`. hyper is driven directly, so no socket or task leaves `alloy-rt`.
- Test certificates come from `rcgen` with `aws_lc_rs` (dev-dependency only). Their throwaway keys sign through the provider. No Alloy key does.
- TLS tests bind loopback ports under `os`, as ADR 0042 allows. The test `NodeStore`/`Access` implement this crate's traits, because `alloy-state` and `alloy-iam` do not exist yet.
- Page token: hex of a canonical prost message holding last name, filter, and order_by. Node ids are `bytes`. Addresses are text.
- Crate-level `allow(clippy::result_large_err)`: the wire `Error` is returned as is.
