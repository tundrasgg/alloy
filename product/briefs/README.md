# Product briefs

One file per brief, `PB-NNNN.md`, written by the product role from
`agent/templates/brief.md`. The lead allocates numbers from `plan/STATE.md`.

A brief describes a user-facing surface from the user's side. The architect turns
an accepted brief into ARFC changes. Briefs are not normative: once an ARFC covers
a brief, the ARFC is the reference.
